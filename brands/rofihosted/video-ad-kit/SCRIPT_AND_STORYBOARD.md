## 🎨 Art Direction & Technical Video Directives

### 1. Color Palette in Video Editing (DaVinci Resolve / Premiere)
* **Lift / Shadows:** Push shadows to `#0a0b0d` (OLED zero-crush). Never allow washed-out muddy grays.
* **Highlights:** Color-grade the lime highlights to strictly match `#c8f56e` and `#a3e635`. Target hue angle: `82° - 84°` (Lime-chartreuse, avoid yellow or cold cyan).
* **Skin Tones:** Keep human hands and reflections natural and slightly warm to balance the cold technology aesthetic.

### 2. Motion Transitions
* **Match Cuts:** Cut from a physical keyboard stroke directly to the digital send button press (`.send-btn`).
* **Push-In:** When zooming into UI components, use `--cc-ease` (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`) with subtle lens motion blur (180° shutter angle).
* **Glass Shimmer:** When displaying cards, animate a diagonal 45° light sweep across `.cc-glass` to show off its backdrop-filter depth.

### 3. Typography Callouts in Video
* All floating text callouts should be set in **Space Grotesk Variable Bold** with letter-spacing `-0.02em`.
* Technical subtext and benchmarks should be set in **JetBrains Mono Variable** with lime accent coloring.
